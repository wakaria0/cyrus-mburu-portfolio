Curious by nature, practical by design.

I like the seam where technology meets business. Most of my work starts the same way: someone has an idea, a service, or a product, and the online side of it isn’t pulling its weight yet.

What I care about are the decisions that are easy to skim past — clear wording, layouts that survive a phone, buttons that say what they do, and pages that load fast on a poor connection. None of that is glamorous, and all of it is the job.
